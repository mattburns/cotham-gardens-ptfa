Play Street & Bake sale this Friday on Cotham Park North!
Come along and buy some sweet treats to raise funds for the school!

We need 2 people at each end of the road at all times, but you don’t have to stay for the whole event. Even if you can just do half an hour that would really help! https://tinyurl.com/2snr9jxh

Y3 are running the Bake Sale. Any donations of baked goods are appreciated - please drop off at the office or bring on the day. NO NUTS please.

Please remember the road will be closed from 3pm to 5pm so do allow some extra time to Park & Stride.
Blue badge holders, residents and emergency vehicles will have access - please drive at walking speed.

Parents are responsible for their children at all times.

Fingers crossed for good weather!
