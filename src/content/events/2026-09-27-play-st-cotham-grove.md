Our first Play St of the academic year is this Friday at the top site. Come along and play straight after school from 3:30-4:30pm. All are welcome! If your child is at the Elmgrove site, please do come up the hill and join.

If you are free to help too please do let us know. If we can get enough volunteers we may be able to sell ice-creams and raise funds for the PTA :)

Cotham Park North will closed to general traffic from 3pm - 4:30pm so please do allow some extra time to park and stride.

Blue badge holders, residents and emergency vehicles will be permitted access. Please drive at a walking speed guided by one of our volunteers.
